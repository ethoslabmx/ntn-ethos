The objectives are:

* Generate quantitative evidence to measure and understand the differentiated impacts of disasters on women, including those related to gender-based violence (as one of the most frequently mentioned effects in the literature review).
* Highlight the need to include this information in the National Disaster Risk Management strategies and share it with key stakeholders.
* Promote evidence-based decision-making with a gender perspective in the development of DRM strategies.
